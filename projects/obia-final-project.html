OBIA Final Project: Coastal Habitat Mapping

Course: Object-Based Image Analysis (OBIA)

A full Object-Based Image Analysis pipeline on SPOT imagery: multi-resolution segmentation, feature extraction, pixel-based SVM classification trained on random samples, and a rule-based classification refinement, compared against one another to produce the final land-cover map.
